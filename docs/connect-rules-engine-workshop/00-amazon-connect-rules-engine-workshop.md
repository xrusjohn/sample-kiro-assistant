# Amazon Connect Rules Engine - Workshop

Amazon Connect Rules Engine - Workshop Amazon Connect  is a self-service, cloud-based contact center service to provide reliable customer service at lower cost. Amazon Connect is based on the same contact center technology used by Amazon customer service around the world powering millions of customer conversations.
In this workshop, you will learn how contact center managers, supervisors and QA analysts can quickly create rules from the Amazon Connect console to automate tasks in Amazon Connect.
With Amazon Connect Rules, contact center managers, supervisors, and quality analysts can automatically categorize contacts, send email notifications or dashboard alerts about customer escalations, prompt tips in agent desktop, and assign tasks for different roles in a contact center. These actions are automated based on conditions that businesses can define such as keywords used on a contact, sentiment trend of a contact, frequent interruptions by agent, agents being silent for long-periods, filtering specific contact attributes or queues, and many more.
Rules also provide third-party integrations with leading CRM vendors such as Salesforce and Zendesk that allows you to trigger a task whenever a new case or ticket is created in Salesforce’s CRM or Zendesk’s ticket management system. You can automatically assign these tasks to agents or supervisors based on the case or ticket created and alert them to take necessary action.
Workshop Overview
Business Challenges
Amazon Connect aims to provide an excellent customer experience and faster issue resolution. Many companies use Amazon Connect across different channels like voice, chat, email, SMS, and video.
Supervisors often face difficulties monitoring real-time metrics, leading to delayed issue identification and resolution, causing longer hold times and lower customer satisfaction scores (CSAT). They need proactive real-time alerts to assess situations quickly, provide assistance, and identify opportunities for agent coaching.
Due to the vast volume of daily contacts, contact center managers struggle to categorize and understand the key reasons for customer interactions. As a result, they face challenges in prioritizing contacts that significantly impact the business and require immediate attention. Manually monitoring dashboards and wallboards is ineffective and error-prone, making it an unreliable approach.
Quality analysts struggle to identify contacts meeting business criteria for evaluation. They spend significant time filtering, selecting contacts, and manually sending follow-up coaching tasks to agents. Most companies cannot perform quality assessments for all customer interactions. They aim to achieve 100% automated agent evaluations to understand sentiment, conversation characteristics, and agent compliance risks.
Proposed Solution
Amazon Connect Rules engine enables contact center managers, supervisors, and quality analysts to set up rules for their specific and unique business use case.
Contact center supervisors can receive real-time notifications about important metric changes through rules. Rules also automatically categorize calls, allowing managers to focus on specific categories of interest. Quality analysts can set rules to determine which contacts undergo automatic evaluation. Based on the evaluation score, coaching tasks can be automatically assigned to agents.
In this workshop we will setup rules to

Notify and take action based on contact center Real-time metrics
Notify and take action based on agent Evaluation result
Notify and take action based on Amazon Connect Case event
Using Conversation analytics rules to

Automatically assign contact category
Alert if the agent's response time is too slow
Detect if agent missed to greet the customer.
